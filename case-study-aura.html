Aura Health —

Designing a patient-facing chronic care management platform that makes complex health journeys feel human, clear, and empowering.

Cover image placeholder

01 — Overview

The problem

Patients managing chronic conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and asthma often navigate fragmented care ecosystems — juggling multiple apps, paper records, and inconsistent provider communication. Aura Health set out to unify this experience into a single, intuitive platform.

My goal was to design an experience that reduces cognitive load, builds trust through clarity, and encourages consistent engagement with care plans.

02 — Research

Understanding the user

I conducted 12 moderated user interviews with patients aged 28–65 managing at least one chronic condition. I also ran a competitive audit of 6 existing health apps and distributed a screener survey to 80+ participants to validate hypotheses.

01

Information overload. 8 of 12 users said they felt overwhelmed by the volume of health data presented to them, leading to app abandonment.

02

Lack of context. Users couldn't understand what their metrics meant without medical training. Data without narrative created anxiety, not clarity.

03

Broken trust cycles. Inconsistent reminders and missed check-ins eroded trust in the system, leading users to disengage within 2–3 weeks.

04

Desire for human connection. Users wanted to feel heard by their care team — not just tracked by an algorithm.

05 — Design

07 — Final Solution

Outcome & takeaways

The final design reduced onboarding drop-off by 38% in pilot testing and increased 30-day retention by 22% compared to the legacy product. The design system built for this project was adopted across two other Aura product lines.

01

Empathy is a design material. The single biggest improvement came from replacing clinical language with conversational copy. Design isn't just visual — it's the words too.

02

Constraints accelerate clarity. Working with strict accessibility requirements (WCAG AA) pushed me to simplify layouts that would have otherwise been overly complex.

03

Test early, test often. Round 1 testing revealed a fundamental IA problem we hadn't anticipated. Catching it at prototype stage saved weeks of engineering rework.
