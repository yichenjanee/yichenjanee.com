Little about Jane ۶ৎ⊹₊⟡⋆

I didn't start out in design. For four years, my world was built on data points and supply chains. As an analyst, I learned how to untangle messy problems, balance the needs of different stakeholders, and let data lead the way. But as much as I valued the logic of it all, I felt a growing pull toward something more personal — I wanted to use that analytical mindset to build things that actually foster human connection.

The turning point was personal. Being diagnosed with anxiety forced me to slow down and reevaluate everything. It changed my perspective on what I wanted my impact to be, grounding my desire to do work that isn't just professionally 'good,' but socially meaningful and empathetic.

That's what led me to Human-Computer Interaction. I'm bridging the gap between strategic analysis and human-centered design to help people solve real-world problems. I'm not just designing interfaces; I'm crafting experiences that are as thoughtful as they are scalable, and as beautiful as they are functional.
